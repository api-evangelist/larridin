In layman’s terms, AI workforce analytics by department and role means measuring AI adoption, proficiency and business impact separately, for each team and each seniority level. It replaces a single company-wide adoption percentage with more actionable data. A single number may tell a board that “78% of employees have AI access.” But that percentage doesn’t tell you whether your support team is resolving tickets faster, or whether your finance team is still building the same spreadsheets by hand, for example.
